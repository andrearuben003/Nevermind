\subsection{Week 1}

\subsubsection{October 6 team meeting}
The team agreed upon the core idea and goal of the game: collecting memories and use them to solve puzzles. \par
Discussed main game mechanics: the team is split between memories being physical objects or items that stay in an intentory of sort (or maybe a hybrid). \par
Discussed the way memories can be remebered and forgotten: the team is undecided between a fixed place or everywhere (this also depends on the way memories work, so that has to be decided first). \par
Discussed upon the idea of \textit{levels}: the team is split between a metroidvania style, based more on exploration, and a hardcore puzzle style, based more on mechanical skill and puzzle solving. \par
Sketched some potential ideas for memories: meterials (like bricks), gravity, yourself (become a ghost), light, dark, time (may be used to pause the game), enemies. Sketched some more ideas like collectibles, bosses (that might unlock memory 'slots'), 'bad' memories (debuffs forced in some cases), frail memories (they can break).
